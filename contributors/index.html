Manoj Kewalramani

Chairperson, Geostrategy Programme · The Takshashila Institution

Manoj Kewalramani is Chairperson of the Geostrategy Programme at the Takshashila Institution. His work focuses on Chinese politics, great power competition, and India’s engagement with international partners amid shifts in the global order. He is the author of Smokeless War: China’s Quest for Geopolitical Dominance and Taming the Dragon: A Manifesto for a New Modus Vivendi with China, and the host of The Great Power Show.

At CrossView

He contributes expert perspective on China–India relations, Chinese political discourse, and the strategic forces shaping Asia.

Mark Anner

Dean and Distinguished Professor · Rutgers School of Management and Labor Relations

Mark Anner is Dean and Distinguished Professor at Rutgers University’s School of Management and Labor Relations. His research focuses on workers’ rights in global supply chains, particularly how sourcing practices, power relations, and labor governance shape working conditions.

He has conducted extensive field research in Latin America, India, Bangladesh, and Vietnam. Before entering academia, he spent twelve years in Central America and Brazil advocating for workers’ rights.

At APEX

He contributes expert perspective on labor rights, purchasing practices, worker voice, and accountability in global garment supply chains.

Sarosh C. Kuruvilla

Andrew J. Nathanson Family Professor in Industrial and Labor Relations · Cornell University ILR School

Sarosh Kuruvilla is the Andrew J. Nathanson Family Professor in Industrial and Labor Relations at Cornell University and Academic Director of the Global Labor Institute. His research examines labor standards, industrial relations, and the effectiveness of private regulation in global supply chains.

His work has explored why corporate labor standards often become disconnected from actual workplace outcomes, as well as the roles of governments, firms, unions, and international institutions in improving worker protections. He is the author of Private Regulation of Labor Standards in Global Supply Chains: Problems, Progress, and Prospects.

At APEX

He contributes expert perspective on labor governance, freedom of association, worker voice, and the limits of private regulation in global supply chains.
