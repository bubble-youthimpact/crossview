#Method

How We Work

Most youth media literacy research is written about young people, not with them.

#Why

Without Ground-Level Reporting

Surveys arrive without context.

Numbers with no reporting behind them to explain what they mean locally.

Findings miss local nuance.

A global figure can flatten very different situations into one line.

Recommendations do not reach students.

Research is written about young people more often than it is shared with them.

Current work

The First Pilot Study

Our first study examines where students find news, which sources they trust, and whether they verify information before sharing it. It runs as a pilot rather than a representative international survey.

Focus
Youth news consumption and verification habits
Method
Correspondent-distributed survey, anonymous and voluntary
Status
In preparation

How We Handle Our Research

  • Sampling is disclosed
  • Participation is voluntary and anonymous
  • Limitations are stated plainly
  • Corrections are public
