Agreement

These Terms of Service govern your use of the CrossView website at crossview-sp.com and any CrossView account. By using the site or creating an account, you agree to these terms and to our Privacy Policy.

Who can use CrossView

CrossView is open to students, educators and anyone interested in media literacy. If you are under 14, you need a parent or guardian’s permission to create an account.

Your account

You are responsible for your account and for keeping your sign-in details secure. Please give accurate information and let us know at once if you think someone else has accessed your account.

Content you submit

You keep ownership of the articles, proposals and other work you submit. By submitting work, you give CrossView permission to edit it for length, clarity and accuracy, and to publish and share it on CrossView and our channels with credit to you.

You confirm that your work is your own, that you have the right to share it, and that you have cited your sources.

Community guidelines

  • Be accurate and fair: represent each country’s coverage honestly and cite your sources.
  • Be respectful: no harassment, hate speech or personal attacks.
  • No plagiarism, and no copying of copyrighted material beyond short, credited quotations.
  • Do not misuse the site, attempt to access other people’s accounts, or interfere with how it works.

We may edit, decline or remove content, or suspend accounts, that break these guidelines.

CrossView materials

Comparisons, infographics, presentations and other materials on CrossView are provided for education. Teachers and students may use and share them for non-commercial, educational purposes with credit to CrossView. Please contact us for any other use.

Third-party links and services

CrossView links to news outlets, Google Drive, Spotify and other services we do not control. Their own terms and privacy policies apply when you use them.

Disclaimer

CrossView presents student analysis of how media frame global events. It is educational content, not professional, legal or political advice. The site is provided “as is”, and we cannot guarantee it will always be available or error-free.

Changes and ending use

We may update these terms from time to time; the date above shows the latest version. You can stop using CrossView and ask us to close your account at any time.

Contact

Questions about these terms? Email crossview.sp@gmail.com.
