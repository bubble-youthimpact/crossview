Using CrossView in Class

A simple four-step lesson flow that works with any comparison.

01

Warm up

Show one headline from each country without labels. Ask students to guess the source.

02

Compare

Open a Cross-View comparison and map tone, emphasis and omitted facts.

03

Discuss

Use a Policy Lab key question to run a structured debate.

04

Reflect

Students write a short perspective piece on how framing changed their view.
