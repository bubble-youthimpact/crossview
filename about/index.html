WHAT IS CROSSVIEW?

About Us

Our vision, mission, and goals

Vision

CrossView exists to strengthen cross-cultural literacy through structured media comparison.

Mission

We equip students to compare how nations report the same events, recognize framing and bias, and engage with global issues from more than one perspective.

The Problem

Students consume global news daily, but rarely compare how different nations frame the same event.

#Why

Without Comparison

Framing

Appears neutral

Bias

Becomes invisible

Narrative

Emphasis goes unquestioned
