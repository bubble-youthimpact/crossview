Overview

CrossView (“CrossView”, “we”, “us”) is a student-led media analysis platform operated at crossview-sp.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or create a Youth Correspondent account, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account or sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. If you sign up with email, we collect your name, email address and a password, which is stored securely by our authentication provider; we never see it in plain text.

Content you submit. Articles, drafts, policy proposals and messages you send through our forms, along with the region and topic information you choose to include.

Contact messages. If you email us or use the contact form, we receive your name, email address and the contents of your message.

Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard information such as browser type, device type and the pages you visit, to keep the site secure and working.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To review, edit and publish the articles and proposals you submit, credited as you choose.
  • To reply to your messages and send you important updates about your account or submissions.
  • To keep the website secure and understand how it is used so we can improve it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google sign-in

If you choose “Continue with Google”, we request only your basic profile information (name, email address and profile picture) to identify you and create your account. We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google data. CrossView’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing your information

We share information only with service providers that help us run CrossView, such as Google Firebase (authentication and data storage) and our website host, and only as needed to provide the service. Published articles show the author name and region you provide. We may disclose information if required by law.

Storage and retention

Your information is stored with our service providers using industry-standard security. We keep account information for as long as your account is active. Published work may remain on the site after you leave unless you ask us to remove it.

Young users

CrossView is designed for students. If you are under 14, please ask a parent or guardian to review this policy and consent before creating an account. If we learn that we have collected information from a child without the required consent, we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or to close your account, at any time by emailing crossview.sp@gmail.com. You can also remove CrossView’s access to your Google account from your Google Account settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date above and, for significant changes, let account holders know.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email crossview.sp@gmail.com.
